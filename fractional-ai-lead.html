Fractional AI Lead: Ongoing AI Governance and Oversight for Growing Teams

Your team already uses AI. What’s missing is one person who owns the rules, keeps an eye on which tools you pay for, and tells you each month what’s working.

Atlas Solutions gives you a named Fractional AI Lead. Chad Keith leads every engagement himself. We stay vendor‑neutral and work with the tools you already have.

Built for companies of about 10 to 150 people.

Discuss a Fractional AI Lead engagement

Who this is for

Someone in charge of AI, without a full-time hire.

Owners and operators who want someone in charge of AI without hiring a full-time AI lead. It usually fits professional services firms, trades, healthcare‑adjacent offices, nonprofits, and growing teams that need steady oversight more than a one-time workshop.

What you get

A named lead, clear rules, and a report each month.

  • A named AI lead — Chad Keith, not a shared inbox.
  • Plain‑English rules — what can go into which tools.
  • Tool and seat advice — which AI tools and seats to keep, and where spend is drifting.
  • Shadow AI checks — tools people use that nobody approved.
  • Monthly reports — aligned to the NIST AI Risk Management Framework.
  • A written scope — so you know what each month covers.

How it works

From intro call to month‑to‑month oversight.

Intro call

We learn your team, your tools, and your concerns.

Written scope

We agree on what the lead covers and how much time it takes each month. Scope is sized to your team and risk level.

First three months

Policy, tool inventory, approved‑tool list, and the first monthly reports. Every engagement starts with a three‑month minimum.

After that

Month to month. Keep going, scale up or down, or stop.

Engagement sizes

Pick the size that matches the team.

Each engagement is scoped in writing. The size follows headcount and how much oversight the work needs.

About 10 to 40 people

Essentials

Clear policy, a short tool inventory, and a monthly check on shadow AI and spend.

About 25 to 80 people

AI Lead

Policy, approved tools, pilot guidance, and monthly NIST AI RMF‑aligned reporting.

About 80 to 150 people

AI Lead Plus

For teams of about 80 to 150, or regulated work. More time for vendor review, controls, and leadership reporting.

Credibility

Governance practice, stated plainly.

Chad Keith is working toward the IAPP AIGP (AI Governance Professional) certification. Monthly reports are aligned to the NIST AI RMF. They are not a NIST certification.

Boundaries

What’s not included.

  • A full-time employee or an on-call help desk
  • Building or hosting your own AI product
  • Reselling AI tool licenses or marking up seats
  • Legal advice or formal compliance certification
  • One-time workshops on their own — see AI Training

FAQ

Straight answers.

What is a fractional AI lead?

A part-time person who owns your AI rules, approves tools, and reports to leadership each month.

How is this different from AI consulting?

Consulting is a set project. A fractional lead is ongoing ownership.

Is there a contract length?

Three months to start, then month to month.
