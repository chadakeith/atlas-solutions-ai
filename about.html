Our story

We’ve done this before.

Atlas Technology Solutions was built in Davidson, North Carolina on a simple promise: bring enterprise‑grade technology practice to businesses that don’t have an enterprise IT department. Over the years that promise has meant Apple deployments, mobile device management as a certified Jamf partner, network builds, managed IT, and cybersecurity for companies across Charlotte and the Carolinas.

Every one of those waves looked the same at the start: powerful new technology, plenty of hype, real risk, and businesses unsure who to trust. Our job has always been the same too — cut through the noise, implement what actually helps, secure it properly, and teach the team to run with it.

AI is the biggest wave yet, and it’s why we built Atlas AI as a dedicated practice: integrations, agentic workflows, training, security, and policy, delivered with the same discipline as everything else we ship.

Why clients pick Atlas for AI

  • We know your environment. Identity, devices, network, data — we manage these layers daily, so AI lands on solid ground.
  • Security isn’t an afterthought. Our cybersecurity practice reviews every integration and agent we build.
  • We stay. As an MSP, our model is long-term operation, not drive-by projects.
  • Plain English, honest advice. If AI isn’t the right answer for a problem, we’ll say so.
