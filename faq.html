Getting started

What is Atlas AI?

Atlas AI is the artificial intelligence practice of Atlas Technology Solutions, an Apple‑certified IT consultancy based in Davidson, NC. We integrate AI into the tools businesses already use, build agentic workflows, train teams, and put the security and policy guardrails in place to adopt AI safely. More about us.

How do we get started?

Email consulting@atlascarolina.com or use the contact form. We start with a conversation about your tools and goals, then propose a scoped first project.

How much does AI consulting cost?

It depends on scope, which is why we always start with a scoped proposal so you know what’s included and what it costs. Most clients begin with a single high‑value project — one integration, one workflow, one policy — so the first investment is modest and the return is measurable.

Is Atlas AI right for small businesses?

Yes — that’s our core audience. We work with companies typically between 10 and 150 people that need practical AI in the tools they already use, not a platform migration. Start with a free AI Readiness Call or browse our scoped offerings for workshops, integrations, and policy starters.

Our team is skeptical of AI. Where should we start?

Start with an executive briefing or a single team workshop. Skepticism usually reflects bad first experiences with generic tools; seeing AI applied carefully to your own real work, with clear guardrails, changes the conversation.

Services

Do we have to replace our existing software?

No. Our whole approach is meeting your stack where it is — we connect AI to the CRM, help desk, documents, and systems you already run, so your team keeps familiar tools and gains new capability.

What exactly is an agentic workflow?

A multi‑step business process executed by an AI agent — triaging inboxes, drafting responses, moving tickets, preparing reports — with human checkpoints where they matter. We design, build, and maintain these for our clients.

Which AI tools and models do you work with?

We’re vendor‑neutral. We evaluate the leading AI platforms and models for each job based on capability, privacy posture, and cost, and we recommend what fits your business — not what pays us a commission.

Do you only work with Apple environments?

No. Apple and Jamf expertise is our heritage and remains a specialty, but AI engagements span whatever your business runs — Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, Windows, and mixed environments included.

Security & governance

Is our company data safe with AI?

It can be — with the right controls. We combine our cybersecurity practice with AI‑specific safeguards: data governance, access controls, vendor evaluation, and a clear acceptable‑use policy, so AI adoption never outpaces your security posture.

Can you write our company’s AI policy?

Yes. AI policy creation is a core Atlas offering — practical, plain‑English acceptable‑use and governance policies tailored to your industry, tooling, and risk tolerance, rolled out with training so they actually stick.

Working with Atlas

Do you work with businesses outside the Charlotte area?

Yes. We’re based in Davidson, NC and serve Charlotte and the Carolinas in person, and AI consulting, training, and integration work is delivered remotely to clients beyond the region.

What happens after a project launches?

We’re a managed service provider at heart, so ongoing operation is our default: monitoring, tuning, and evolving integrations and agents as your business and the technology change. One‑time engagements are available too.
