Who we are

This site is operated by Atlas Technology Solutions (“Atlas,” “we,” “us”) for the Atlas AI practice. Contact: consulting@atlascarolina.com.

What we collect

  • Contact and subscribe forms — name, email, phone, company, message, and any interest you select (for example, a readiness call).
  • Analytics — if enabled, Google Analytics 4 and/or Microsoft Clarity may collect usage data such as pages viewed, clicks, and approximate location. We configure these tools to respect privacy where options exist (for example, IP anonymization in GA4).
  • Server logs — GitHub Pages and our DNS provider may log IP addresses and browser types for security and operations.

How we use it

  • Respond to inquiries and schedule consultations.
  • Send blog updates if you subscribe (you can unsubscribe by replying to any message).
  • Understand how visitors use the site so we can improve content and the funnel.
  • Protect the site from abuse.

We do not sell your personal information.

Third-party services

Form submissions are processed by FormSubmit. Analytics, if configured, are provided by Google and/or Microsoft. Each provider processes data under its own terms.

Your choices

You may request access, correction, or deletion of information you submitted by emailing consulting@atlascarolina.com. You can disable analytics in your browser or use browser extensions that block tracking.
