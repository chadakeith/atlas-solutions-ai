Atlas AI / Case studies / Corporate AI training

Corporate AI training that sticks.

Home services company · ~20 employees · Carolinas · AI Training

The challenge

The owner had tried a generic online AI course. Leadership was curious; field and office staff were skeptical. Nobody felt sold to — they needed proof on their estimates, schedules, and customer messages, not demos on someone else’s business.

Our approach

Atlas AI designed a corporate training program in three parts: a leadership briefing on realistic ROI and risk, a half‑day hands‑on workshop split by role (office vs operations), and written playbooks each person could keep. Sessions used their real tools — email, documents, and field‑update templates — with clear rules on customer data and approvals.

We followed with monthly office hours for the first quarter so questions surfaced after real use, not just in the classroom. That reinforcement pattern is standard in our corporate AI training engagements.

Results

Attendees left with automations and prompts they used the same week — proposal outlines, job‑summary drafts, and safer customer‑communication patterns. The owner said it was the first AI session where the team understood what they could and couldn’t do, without a hype deck.
