The reality

Shadow AI is the new shadow IT.

In most businesses, employees adopted AI before leadership decided anything: pasting customer emails into free chatbots, uploading spreadsheets to summarizers, connecting browser extensions with sweeping permissions. Every one of those moments is a data governance decision made by default — and invisibly.

Locking AI out doesn’t work; it just pushes usage further into the shadows and hands your competitors the productivity gains. The answer is governed adoption: sanctioned tools that are genuinely good, clear rules people can follow, technical controls that make the safe path the easy path, and monitoring that verifies all of it.

This is where Atlas is most at home. Security isn’t a feature we added for the AI era — it’s the practice we’ve built alongside managed IT and device management for years. AI security extends that same discipline to a new surface.

How it works

Assess, control, verify, repeat.

Assess

An AI security review: current usage, data exposure, vendor risk, and gaps — delivered as a prioritized, plain-English report.

Control

Sanctioned tools configured securely, risky flows blocked or redirected, agent permissions scoped and logged.

Verify

Testing and monitoring confirm the controls hold in practice — including the human layer, via safe-use training.

Repeat

Quarterly reviews as tools, models, and threats change. AI security is a posture, not a project.
