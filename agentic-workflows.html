The shift

From “AI helps me do it” to “AI did it.”

The first wave of workplace AI was assistive: a person asks, the AI answers, the person does the work. Agentic workflows are the second wave — the AI carries the process itself. An agent can read an incoming request, look up the account, draft the response, update the record, schedule the follow‑up, and hand a human the one decision that actually needs a human.

Done carelessly, that’s a liability. Done well, it’s the biggest capacity gain a small or mid‑size business can buy. The difference is design: which steps the agent owns, which require approval, what it can never touch, and how every action is logged. That design discipline — borrowed from a decade of managing production IT — is what Atlas brings.

We build on proven automation and AI platforms rather than science projects, we start with one workflow instead of twenty, and we instrument everything so you can see exactly what your agents did and why.

What’s included

A complete workflow engagement.

  • Process mapping — we document the workflow as it really happens, not as the org chart says.
  • Agent design — steps, decision points, approval gates, and escalation paths on paper before anything runs.
  • Guardrails — hard limits on what the agent can access and do, aligned with your security policy.
  • Build & pilot — the agent runs in shadow mode first; you compare its work to yours before it acts alone.
  • Human checkpoints — approvals inserted exactly where judgment, risk, or customer trust demands them.
  • Full audit trail — every action logged and reviewable.
  • Handoff & training — your team learns to supervise, adjust, and extend the workflow.
  • Managed operation — we monitor and tune agents in production, as long as you want us to.

How it works

Crawl, walk, run — deliberately.

Map

We shadow the real process and find the steps that are rule-based, repetitive, and measurable — the agent sweet spot.

Design

You approve the agent’s job description: what it does, what it asks permission for, what it never touches.

Shadow

The agent runs alongside your team without acting. We compare outputs until accuracy earns autonomy.

Release

Autonomy expands step by step, with logs, metrics, and a human override always one click away.
